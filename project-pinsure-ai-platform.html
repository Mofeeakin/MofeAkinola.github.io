Pinsure - AI Insurance Platform

Multi-agent insurance workflows for underwriting, claims, and fraud analysis.

Project Overview

Pinsure is a multi-agent AI platform designed to automate insurance workflows from policy intake to claims evaluation. The system improves processing speed while preserving modular control over decision paths.

Problem Context

Insurance teams often process underwriting and claims tasks across disconnected systems and manual handoffs. This creates delays, inconsistent decisions, and limited visibility across policy and claims pipelines.

System Design

  • Data sources: policy submissions, claims records, customer attributes, and risk rules
  • Processing layer: agent orchestration pipeline for intake, validation, and scoring
  • AI components: specialized decision agents for underwriting logic and fraud checks
  • Backend services: FastAPI REST endpoints for claims and policy operations
  • Frontend interaction: Builder.io and React interfaces for workflow review and actioning

Key Capabilities

  • Automated claims intake
  • Policy decision workflows
  • Fraud detection logic
  • Real-time API-based processing

Tech Stack

Python, FastAPI, Docker, React, Builder.io

Outcome / Value

Pinsure reduces manual processing time, standardizes decision handling, and enables scalable insurance operations through modular services and deployment-ready architecture.
