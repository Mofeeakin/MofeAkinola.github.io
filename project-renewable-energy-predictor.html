Renewable Energy Predictor

Demand forecasting platform for renewable energy planning across sectors.

Project Overview

A forecasting system designed to predict renewable energy demand across economic sectors. It combines time-series and regression modeling with interactive analytics for scenario exploration.

Problem Context

Energy planning teams need reliable demand projections to support infrastructure and policy decisions. Without robust forecasts, planning cycles can miss demand shifts and reduce allocation efficiency.

System Design

  • Data sources: historical consumption records, sector activity indicators, and trend variables
  • Processing layer: data cleaning, aggregation, and feature preparation for temporal modeling
  • AI components: regression and time-series models for demand prediction
  • Backend services: analytics workflow packaging for repeatable scenario runs
  • Frontend interaction: Power BI dashboards for assumptions and projection comparison

Key Capabilities

  • Sector-level demand forecasting
  • Scenario-based analysis
  • Data-driven planning support

Tech Stack

Python, Scikit-learn, Pandas, Power BI

Outcome / Value

The predictor provides structured evidence for planning and policy decisions, helping teams compare sector pathways and prepare resource allocation strategies with clearer demand visibility.
