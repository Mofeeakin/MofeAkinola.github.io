Oil & Gas Intelligence Platform

Operational monitoring, ESG tracking, and geospatial risk analytics in one system.

Project Overview

A geospatial and analytics system for monitoring production, environmental risk, and operational performance. The platform unifies fragmented operational data and makes it accessible through dashboards and natural language querying.

Problem Context

Energy operations often rely on disconnected tools for production metrics, incidents, weather, and location-based risk. This fragmentation slows response time and reduces decision quality during operational planning.

System Design

  • Data sources: production records, incident logs, ESG indicators, and geospatial boundary layers
  • Processing layer: ETL workflows to normalize and join operational and location data
  • AI components: RAG pipeline to support natural language analysis of structured metrics
  • Backend services: FastAPI endpoints for analytics retrieval and map-based insights
  • Frontend interaction: dashboard and map views for operational and risk exploration

Key Capabilities

  • Natural language data querying
  • Geospatial risk heatmaps
  • ESG and incident tracking
  • Weather-linked operational insights

Tech Stack

Python, FastAPI, RAG, ArcGIS, SQL

Outcome / Value

The platform improves visibility into operational risk, shortens time-to-insight, and supports faster planning decisions across production and compliance workflows.
